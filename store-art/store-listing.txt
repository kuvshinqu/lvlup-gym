SHORT DESCRIPTION (max 80)

FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000)
Your training is the quest. Your hero gets stronger every time you do.

Level Up Gym is a workout tracker built like an RPG. Log a set and your hero earns XP. Train a muscle group and its skill levels up. Lift more than ever before and you claim a breakthrough, with bonus XP.

FORGE YOUR HERO
- Earn XP for every set, cardio session and finished workout
- Raise seven skills, one for each part of your body: chest, back, legs, shoulders, arms, core and stamina
- Watch your hero level up, with a level-up moment worth the sweat

FOLLOW A PROVEN QUEST
- A 4-day upper/lower program, built on strength training research
- Weekly quest: finish 4 workouts in a week for bonus XP
- Or train your own way and add your own exercises

LEARN EVERY MOVE
- Each exercise has an animated drawing of your hero, from start to finish
- The working muscles glow, so you know what you are training
- Short form notes for safe, clean reps

TRACK WHAT MATTERS
- Weight and reps for lifts, time and distance for cardio
- Your chronicle: every workout, every set, every personal best
- Body weight log with a 30-day trend
- Kilograms or pounds, kilometres or miles

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no sign-up, no internet connection needed. Your training data stays on your phone.

Pick up the bar. Your hero is waiting.
